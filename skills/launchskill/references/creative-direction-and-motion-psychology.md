# Creative Direction, Motion Psychology & Anti-Template Choreography Guide

Why do 95% of AI-generated Remotion launch videos feel like lifeless, interchangeable "template slop"—and how do top motion studios (**Ordinary Folk**, **ManvsMachine**, **Buck**), master educators (**Ben Marriott**, **Jorge R. Canedo E.**), design engineers (**Emil Kowalski**, **Rauno Freiberg**), and category-defining product teams (**Apple**, **Linear**, **Raycast**, **Arc**, **Runway**, and top launches on **What Ships**) make 20–45 seconds of software feel **alive, bespoke, and impossible to scroll past**?

This guide is the **Creative Brain** of `launchskill`. Read it **before** designing any storyboard so you never paste a product into a rigid slide-deck template.

---

## 0. The 4 Golden Laws of Visual-First Launch Videos (Move Viewers With Design & Illustration, Never Text Manuals)

A launch video is **motion design**, not a user manual. If a viewer has to *read* paragraphs, chat bubbles, or dense tables to understand your video, you have already lost them.

1. **Ban Chat Bubbles & Typed Prompt Sentences (Instant AI Slop Tell):**
   - Never open a video—or stage a workflow—with a chat bubble or prompt input box typing out a 15-word sentence (*"Just landed in Tokyo — which track is the Narita Express..."* or fake iMessage threads).
   - Every generic AI wrapper video uses chat bubbles. Instead, represent voice and intelligence through **sculptural visual form**: a glowing 3D sonic iris, a bi-directional harmonic equalizer, or a rotating orbital constellation that physically morphs into the device.
2. **Ban Skeleton Wireframe Grids & Giant Background Watermark Words:**
   - Never tile `1px` wireframe boxes across the backdrop (looks like an unfinished CAD skeleton), and never place giant faint watermark words (`"LIVE"`, `"VISION"`, `"ACTION"`) behind the hero object. They clutter the silhouette and distract the eye.
   - Use pure, seamless tonal backdrops so 100% of visual contrast belongs to the sculptural foreground objects.
3. **Replace Text Tables & Explanatory Paragraphs With Tactile Visual Illustrations (Max 1–3 Words per Beat):**
   - Never put left-side explanatory paragraphs (*"Gemini reads departure boards, menus, and diagrams..."*) or 15-string schedule tables on screen.
   - **Translate every concept into a pure visual widget or illustration:**
     - *Instead of a text table about rescheduling a 7 PM dinner to 8 PM:* Show a **tactile circular watch dial** where the hands physically sweep from `7:00` to `8:00` and lock emerald green (`LOCKED ✓`).
     - *Instead of a text description of camera vision:* Show a **rich layered vector artwork inside the camera lens** (sunset ridge, architectural arch monolith, rotating optical focus ring, sweeping laser scan beam) that explodes a **visual color-swatch & miniature icon card** `+88px` out of the glass!
     - *Instead of a text paragraph about navigation:* Show a **stylized topographic vector map** where a glowing blue S-curve route draws itself to a bouncing emerald destination pin.
4. **Continuous Object Permanence & Transformative Transitions:**
   - Connect every act through physical transformation: **Sonic Iris Capsule → 3D Container Morph into Tilted Phone → Z-Axis Card Explosion → 2.5D Perspective Whip-Flip → Portal Zoom-Through Match Cut into the 3D Glass Emblem**.

## 0.5 The Autonomous Creative Brain: Design From Scratch (Ban the "Widget Buffet")

> **Core Principle:** Pre-built widgets (`VisualWidgets.tsx`) are **physics references**, NOT a sticker pack to dump onto every screen. An expert motion director invents a **bespoke, unified visual world from scratch** tailored strictly to the product's actual story.

1. **Ban the "Widget Buffet" (1 Focal Subject Per Beat + 75% Calm Negative Space):**
   - Never stack 3+ unrelated template components (e.g., a spinner + a pill splitter + dangling yellow wires + a glass capsule) in the same shot. That creates visual chaos and forces the viewer to decode what they're looking at.
   - **Rule:** Every shot gets **ONE primary focal subject** and **ONE clear semantic action** surrounded by **70–85% calm, matte negative space** (warm archival paper `#F7F7F5` or pure obsidian `#0A0A0C`).
2. **Ban Dangling Decorative Wires & Orphan Geometry:**
   - If you draw a connector line, arrow, or Bezier curve, it **MUST physically originate at Object A and terminate at Object B**. Never shoot decorative yellow or blue lines out into empty whitespace pointing at nothing.
3. **Ban Forced 2.5D Tilt on Flat Editorial Typography:**
   - Do **NOT** wrap every shot in `<LivingPerspectiveWorkstation />` tilting flat text `-18° → +18°` with a sweeping blue diagonal gradient behind it. That looks like a generic 3D PowerPoint template.
   - Reserve 2.5D perspective strictly for genuine physical hardware (phones, laptops, layered 3D cards). Editorial typography (`Paper`, `Clanker UI`, `Interface Lineage`) belongs **optically flat, razor-sharp, and dead-centered on a calm matte canvas** — animated purely through timing, masking, scale, and physical continuity.
4. **Ban Generic "AI Dashboard" Clichés:**
   - Never drop a fake macOS traffic-light code box (`const res = await product.run()`) next to a fake `97 SCORE` radial donut gauge when the product isn't an IDE or analytics monitor.
   - Instead, **invent bespoke domain-native visuals from scratch**:
     - For a **video creation skill (`launchskill`)**: build a custom minimalist **video timeline with a live playhead scrubbing across real beat tracks (`HOOK`, `132 BPM`, `-14 LUFS`) and a live audio waveform**.
     - For a **fintech / ledger app**: build a custom live balance settlement rail.
     - For a **design tool**: build a custom vector canvas & live token inspector.
5. **Optical Typography Continuity:**
   - During a glyph-scramble or text morph, keep the **exact same font metrics / bounding width** (`fontVariantNumeric: "tabular-nums"` or smooth cross-dissolve inside a fixed optical anchor) so the word never jumps jaggedly from monospace to sans-serif mid-transition.
6. **Ban "PowerPoint / Magic Move" Slow Box Resizing (Real Camera Choreography & Velocity Contrast):**
   - Never build a 15-second film by resizing one centred box's `width` and `height` every 4 seconds at one uniform medium speed—that reads like a Keynote *Magic Move* deck, not cinema.
   - Drive the frame with **real virtual camera choreography** (`scale(2.2)` macro close-ups off-centre → `180–250ms` exponential pull-back to `1.0×` wide → brisk `0.8–1.4s` kinetic proof cuts alternating with slow, continuous camera drift).
   - **Show, don't label:** Never waste a climax beat on static labelled progress bars (`CAMERA / TYPE / AUDIO`); demonstrate the product's real output directly inside the locked visual world.
7. **The 15-Second Stranger Test (Story Clarity Over Motion Tricks):**
   - Before writing any animation, write the entire video's story in **3 plain sentences (`Hook → Product Proof → CTA`)**.
   - **Never cut full-screen into an unrelated fake product or random numbers** just to hit a motion effect. If a muted stranger scrolling X can't explain what the product does after one watch, rewrite the story immediately.

## 2. The Cognitive Psychology of High-Retention Launch Videos

Great motion design is applied perceptual psychology. On X (Twitter), a viewer's thumb is already moving when your video enters the viewport. You have **250 milliseconds** to arrest motor action, **2.5 seconds** to establish a spatial mental model, and **15–40 seconds** to sustain dopamine prediction errors without causing cognitive fatigue.

```
0ms ────── 250ms ────────── 2.5s ──────────────────── 15s–40s
 │           │               │                          │
 └─ Pre-Attentive Capture    └─ Spatial Mental Model    └─ Dopamine Rhythm & Proof
    (1 Bold Motion Vector,      (Contextual Origin,        (Variable Pacing, Match Cuts,
     Scale Violation,            Object Permanence,         Tactile Audio-Visual Lock)
     Figure-Ground Depth)        Immediate Product Proof)
```

### 2.1 Pre-Attentive Visual Capture (`0 – 250ms`: Stopping the Scroll)
Before the conscious cortex reads a single word, the human primary visual cortex (V1) processes four pre-attentive channels in parallel: **Motion Vector Singularity**, **Scale Violation**, **Luminance Contrast**, and **Depth Occlusion**.
- **Why "Center Headline + 4 Orbiting Cards" Fails Pre-Attentive Capture:** When 5 objects sit evenly distributed across the frame with equal contrast (like `hero-01.png` in `GeminiMobileLaunch`), attention splits 5 ways. The eye has no single entry point.
- **How to Win Frame 0:**
  - **One Dominant Focal Object with Scale Drama:** Open on an extreme macro close-up (`2.5×–3.5×`) of the product in action, a bold 2.5D tilted device breaking the frame edges, or a high-contrast kinetic word physically interacting with the hero object.
  - **Figure-Ground Depth Occlusion:** Layer elements across **3 distinct Z-planes** right at Frame 0 (e.g., giant bold typography on the back plane `Z = -120px`, the tilted physical phone/window on the hero mid-plane `Z = 0px` partially overlapping the text, and a crisp live UI card floating in front of the bezel at `Z = +60px`). The brain instantly perceives real 3D space instead of a flat banner image.

### 2.2 Gestalt Laws in Motion: Continuity, Common Fate & Object Permanence
The human visual system evolved to track **persistent physical objects moving through continuous space**—not discrete slide cuts.
1. **Object Permanence (The Anti-Slide Law):**
   - In the real world, objects do not vanish at frame 83 and get replaced by a new layout at frame 84.
   - When Scene A's hero element **physically morphs, rotates, or zooms through** to become Scene B's canvas (a *Shared-Element Container Transform* or *Velocity Match Cut*), the viewer's visual working memory stays unbroken. They feel effortless flow instead of jarring resets.
2. **Contextual Origin (Emil Kowalski & Rauno Freiberg's Spatial Anchoring Principle):**
   - *"An object must emerge from what triggered it."*
   - If a user taps a button at `(x: 480, y: 770)` or a camera reticle locks onto an object at `(x: 960, y: 420)`, the resulting answer card or action sheet must expand directly from that exact `(x, y)` origin (`transformOrigin`), pushing neighboring UI aside smoothly.
3. **Foveal Singularity (`2°` Visual Angle Rule):**
   - The human fovea can only resolve high-detail text within a **`2°` cone** (~`220×220px` on a desktop monitor, or one thumb-width on mobile).
   - **Never animate two competing text blocks in different screen corners at the same time.** Guide the eye along a continuous spatial thread: where Action A finishes (`x: 960, y: 540`) is the exact coordinate where Action B begins!

### 2.3 Dopamine Prediction Error & Asymmetric Rhythm
If every scene lasts exactly `4.0 seconds` and transitions the exact same way, the brain predicts the pattern by second 8 and disengages.
- **Variable Cadence (The "Accordion" Rhythm):**
  - Alternate **high-density kinetic bursts** (`0.35s – 0.75s` rapid match-cuts, multi-angle product snaps, rhythmic word hits) with **luxurious, breathing hero demonstrations** (`3.5s – 5.5s` continuous camera moves where a real workflow unfolds from trigger to payoff).
- **Scale & Perspective Contrast Between Adjacent Shots:**
  - Never cut from a `1.0×` medium shot to another `1.0×` medium shot.
  - Follow the **Cinematographer's 30° / 2× Rule**: Every cut or camera move must change either **scale by at least `1.6×`** (Macro `2.4×` ↔ Wide `0.9×`) or **camera angle by at least `25°`** (Orthographic front ↔ 2.5D Isometric tilt).

### 2.4 Synchresis (Michel Chion's Audio-Visual Fusion Law)
Coined by film theorist Michel Chion, **synchresis** is the involuntary neurological fusion that happens when a crisp acoustic transient (`click`, `sub-drop`, `whoosh peak`, `marimba resolve`) occurs within **`±1 frame (33ms)`** of a visual velocity inflection point.
- The sound gives **physical mass** to flat pixels. A UI card snapping into place with a `3ms` damped low-mid body transient feels made of solid aluminum and glass; without it (or misaligned by 6 frames), it feels like weightless CSS.

## 3. Masterclass Lessons from the World's Best Motion Creators & Studios

### 3.1 Ben Marriott & Ordinary Folk (Jorge R. Canedo E.): Match-Cut Choreography & Elastic Follow-Through
- **The Velocity Match Cut:** To transition between two completely different scenes without a cheesy wipe:
  1. Drive the focal shape in Scene A (e.g., a circular camera lens, a rounded phone screen, a pill button) to accelerate along a clear vector (`scale: 1 → 4.5` or `translateX: 0 → -600px`, using `EASE_IN_EXPO`).
  2. Cut at the **exact frame of peak velocity**—never let the outgoing object slow down before the cut!
  3. On frame 0 of Scene B, place the new object (which shares the same silhouette, position, or movement axis) already moving at that peak velocity, and decelerate it smoothly (`EASE_OUT_EXPO` or `SPRING_EDITORIAL`). The brain fuses the two shots into one impossible, magical transformation.
- **Overlapping Action & Follow-Through (Killing Rigid-Block Motion):**
  - Never animate a complex component (like a phone or a card) as a single frozen `<div style={{ transform }}>`.
  - Break every hero object into **3–4 internal hierarchy layers** separated by `2–4 frames` (`0.06–0.12s`):
    - **Layer 1 (Chassis / Outer Shell):** Leads the motion (`t = 0`).
    - **Layer 2 (Header / Media Canvas):** Follows at `t + 2f` with `1.05×` internal parallax slide.
    - **Layer 3 (Primary Typography / Waveform):** Follows at `t + 4f` with a masked baseline rise.
    - **Layer 4 (Badges / Micro-Controls):** Settles last at `t + 6f` with a crisp `SPRING_POP` overshoot.

### 3.2 ManvsMachine & Apple Motion Design: 2.5D Hardware Staging, Specular Light & Depth Occlusion
Watch any Apple iPhone / macOS / WWDC trailer or ManvsMachine product film:
- **Devices Move Like Sculptural Objects in 3D Space:**
  - Even in pure 2D React/CSS without WebGL overhead, wrapping a device or UI window in a `perspective: 1400px` stage and driving continuous `rotateX`, `rotateY`, `rotateZ`, and `translateZ` curves makes it feel tangible and expensive.
- **Coupled Specular Glass Sheen:**
  - What sells the 3D tilt isn't just the perspective matrix—it's the **light reacting to the rotation**.
  - Add a diagonal specular sheen overlay (`linear-gradient(115deg, transparent 35%, rgba(255,255,255,0.18) 48%, rgba(255,255,255,0.03) 54%, transparent 70%)`) whose `translateX` is mathematically driven by `rotateY * 14px`. As the phone tilts left to right, studio light sweeps across its OLED glass and titanium chamfer!
- **Dynamic Contact & Elevation Shadows:**
  - Couple the shadow offset (`boxShadow` or a blurred ground-plane shadow oval) inversely to the tilt angle (`shadowX = -rotateY * 1.8`, `shadowY = 36 + rotateX * 1.5`) so the object feels suspended above a real physical surface.

### 3.3 Emil Kowalski & Rauno Freiberg: Mass-Proportional Physics & Organic Timing
- **Duration Scales With Mass & Distance ("Truck vs. Bicycle"):**
  - Micro-interactions (button press, toggle flip, status dot): **`120ms – 180ms`** (`SPRING_SNAPPY`, low mass `0.6`).
  - Medium UI transitions (action sheet expanding from a button, card morphing): **`240ms – 340ms`** (`SPRING_EDITORIAL`, mass `0.9`).
  - Macro camera dives & 2.5D device rotations across the stage: **`550ms – 850ms`** (`EASE_IN_OUT_QUINT` or `SPRING_SMOOTH`, mass `1.3`).
  - *When every element in a video uses the exact same spring constant, everything feels made of the same plastic.* Vary mass across hierarchy!
- **Harmonic Staggering (Banning Robotic Linear Loops):**
  - Instead of `delay = idx * 0.12`, use a compressing curve (`delay = Math.pow(idx, 0.78) * 0.08`) so lists accelerate like a domino cascade rather than ticking like a metronome.

### 3.4 Linear, Raycast, Arc & Runway: Visual Metaphor & Screen-to-World Breakout
- **Abstract-to-Concrete Opening:**
  - Don't open with a literal corporate badge (`"GEMINI MOBILE"`). Open with the **sensory phenomenon** of the product:
    - For a **Voice + Vision Mobile AI**: Open on a living, breathing luminous sound/light wave or camera iris that reacts to a spoken question and physically *frames* itself into a sleek titanium smartphone.
    - For a **Command Launcher / DevTool**: Open on a razor-sharp keystroke beam or splitting code branch that expands into the workspace.
    - For a **Creative Canvas**: Open inside a full-bleed finished artwork, then pull the camera back *through* the artboard frame to reveal 15 artboards being orchestrated on an infinite canvas.
- **Screen-to-World Breakout (2.5D Exploded UI Layers):**
  - Don't trap all UI flat inside a `436×886` phone box where text becomes tiny!
  - Have the active UI cards (the live camera detection reticle, the voice transcript, the Gmail/Calendar/Maps cards) **physically pop out of the phone screen along the Z-axis (`translateZ(60px..140px)`)**, floating 20% larger above the glass with parallax depth shadows as the phone tilts! That solves mobile readability *and* creates jaw-dropping visual craft at the same time.

## 4. The Mandatory 5-Question Creative Director Protocol (Run BEFORE Writing Any Code)

**STOP.** Never copy an existing composition file (`T3CodeLaunch.tsx`, `SaasLaunch.tsx`, etc.) and replace its text strings. Before writing a single line of Remotion code, explicitly answer and design around these **5 Creative Direction Questions**:

### Question 1: What is the Product's Native Habitat & Sensory World?
Do not default blindly to a white dot-grid cyclorama if it doesn't fit the product's soul. Choose a **Bespoke World Architecture**:

| Product Soul | Native Visual World | Camera & Staging Signature |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Consumer Mobile App / AI Companion** *(e.g. Gemini Mobile, Arc Search, Duolingo, Spotify)* | Tactile 2.5D device choreography, rich atmospheric color washes or crisp daylight-to-OLED contrast, **Exploded Z-Layer UI cards popping out of the phone screen**, giant depth-occluded typography behind the tilted phone. | 2.5D perspective device rig (`rotateX/Y/Z`) + coupled glass sheen + screen-to-world card breakout + portal zoom-throughs. |
| **Developer Tool / IDE / Infra / CLI** *(e.g. Cursor, Linear, Raycast, Vercel, T3 Code)* | Precision dark obsidian (`#09090B`) OR high-contrast dark macOS window (`#0D0D10`) over architectural paper, `1px` specular hairlines, real multi-token code/diff choreography, `⌘K` origin morphs. | Orthographic precision + razor-fast `<SpatialCameraRig>` macro dives (`1.0× → 2.4×`) onto active tokens/diffs. |
| **Spatial Creative Suite / Node / Ad Canvas** *(e.g. Figma, Runway, Fuser, Hera)* | Infinite spatial canvas where media itself is the star—multi-aspect live video/image artboards, radial blooms, tactile direct-manipulation sliders, full-bleed commercial zoom-outs. | High-velocity `<SpatialCameraRig>` whip-pans (`blurX/Y`) + canvas-to-full-bleed scale-through match cuts. |
| **Editorial / Research / Knowledge / Swiss Index** *(e.g. Perplexity, Notion, What Ships)* | High-contrast editorial typography (`Instrument Serif` / `Geist` / `JetBrains Mono`), `<ViewfinderFrame>` reticles, full-bleed retrieval strobes, `<RollingOdometer>` counters, tactile paper-and-ink craft. | Hard-cut `3.2×` macro punch-ins + architectural `clip-path` curtain wipes + baseline-masked kinetic type slates. |
| **Physical Hardware / Wearable / Industrial** *(e.g. Apple Watch, Teenage Engineering, Nothing)* | Persistent `@remotion/three` `<ThreeCanvas>` with offline `RoomEnvironment` PMREM studio softbox reflections, orbital camera sweeps, `1px` SVG macro callout leader lines. | Continuous 3D orbital camera + light-to-dark studio rim-light transitions. |

### Question 2: What is the "Through-Line Hero Object" (Object Permanence)?
Identify **one visual anchor** that transforms continuously across at least 3–4 scenes so the film feels like **one continuous choreography**, not 6 slides:
- *Example (Gemini Mobile):* **The Living Gemini Prompt / Voice Pill** → starts Frame 0 as a giant macro glowing voice/prompt bar across the center of the screen → **morphs its container** into the bottom bar of a 2.5D tilted titanium smartphone → pops an exploded Z-layer Vision Answer Card **out of the glass** → **zooms through** the card into a 3D fanned stack of live Gmail/Calendar/Maps cards floating above the angled phone → collapses into the final geometric Gemini mark.

### Question 3: How Do We Show Rich Visual Proof Without LLM Stick-Figure Clipart?
**Rule:** *Never write raw SVG stick-drawings of real-world objects (coffee grinders, bicycles, people, sneakers).*
Instead, build visual proof from materials that React/SVG renders at **10/10 studio quality**:
1. **Rich Layered UI & Exploded Glass Cards** (authentic mobile/desktop chrome, real message threads, interactive maps with vector street grids + route polylines + live ETA pins, calendar time-block schedules morphing live, audio waveform visualizers).
2. **Real Photographic / Video Assets (`<Img>` / `<OffthreadVideo>`)** when available in `public/`, OR **Architectural / Data / Topographic / Shader Surfaces** (rich CSS multi-stop mesh lighting, topographic contour maps, live terminal/code editors, interactive flight/itinerary passes, studio boarding passes, rich music album players).
3. **2.5D Perspective Hardware with Specular Glass Reflection** where UI layers float in 3D space above the screen (`transformStyle: "preserve-3d"`, `translateZ(50px)`).

### Question 4: What Are the 4 Distinct Transition Mechanics Across the Film?
**Ban repeating the same transition twice in a row, and ban plain `<Sequence>` hard-cuts where a static screen just disappears.** Pick at least 3–4 distinct physical transitions across the edit:
1. **Shared-Container Morph (`layoutMorph`)**: Element A in Scene 1 physically interpolates its `width`, `height`, `borderRadius`, `x`, `y`, and `backgroundColor` into Element B in Scene 2.
2. **Portal Zoom-Through Match Cut**: The camera pushes *inside* an active card, viewfinder, or button (`scale: 1 → 5.5` with `EASE_IN_EXPO`) until that element's interior becomes the full `1920×1080` background of the next scene (`scale: 0.85 → 1.0` with `EASE_OUT_EXPO`).
3. **2.5D Perspective Whip-Turn**: The tilted 3D device rotates rapidly across its Y/X axis (`rotateY: -18° → +90°` out, `-90° → +16°` in) with coupled specular flash and directional blur, flipping from one workflow to the next.
4. **Architectural Split-Curtain / Kinetic Type Push**: Incoming giant kinetic typography or a full-bleed color plane physically pushes the outgoing composition off-axis with secondary parallax.

### Question 5: Is Every "Hold" Alive With Micro-Momentum?
During any `1.0–2.0s` readability window:
- The **text being read** stays crisp (`0px` blur) and legible.
- The **stage** never freezes: keep a gentle 2.5D perspective float (`rotateY` drifting `3° → 6°`, `rotateX` drifting `4° → 2°`), a moving specular glass highlight across the phone bezel, live reactive waveform bars or progress indicators, and subtle multi-plane parallax.

## 5. Production-Ready Recipes for Living Motion in Remotion

### Recipe 1: 2.5D Perspective Device Stage + Coupled Specular Glass Sheen + Exploded Z-Layers
Instead of a flat 2D phone rectangle, use CSS `perspective` and `preserve-3d` so the phone tilts in 3D space, light sweeps across the glass, and focal UI cards **float 40–90px in front of the screen** with real depth shadows!

```tsx
export const PerspectivePhoneStage: React.FC<{
  rotateX: number; // e.g. 8 to -4 deg
  rotateY: number; // e.g. -22 to 16 deg
  rotateZ?: number; // e.g. -2 to 2 deg
  scale?: number;
  x?: number;
  y?: number;
  sheenProgress?: number; // 0 -> 1 sweeps studio light across the glass
  explodedOverlay?: React.ReactNode; // Cards floating at translateZ(+75px) in front of the glass!
  behindText?: React.ReactNode;      // Giant kinetic type at translateZ(-140px) behind the phone!
  children: React.ReactNode;
}> = ({
  rotateX,
  rotateY,
  rotateZ = 0,
  scale = 1,
  x = 0,
  y = 0,
  sheenProgress = 0.5,
  explodedOverlay,
  behindText,
  children,
}) => {
  // Dynamic shadow shifts opposite to device tilt
  const shadowX = -rotateY * 2.2;
  const shadowY = 42 + rotateX * 1.8;
  const sheenOffset = (sheenProgress - 0.5) * 760 - rotateY * 14;

  return (
    <div
      style={{
        position: "absolute",
        inset: 0,
        perspective: 1400,
        display: "flex",
        alignItems: "center",

        pointerEvents: "none",
      }}
    >
      {/* Back Z-Plane (-140px): Giant Depth-Occluded Kinetic Typography */}
      {behindText && (
        <div
          style={{
            position: "absolute",
            inset: 0,
            display: "flex",
            alignItems: "center",
            justifyContent: "center",
            transform: `translate3d(${-rotateY * 1.4}px, ${-rotateX * 1.1}px, -140px)`,
          }}
        >
          {behindText}
        </div>
      )}

      {/* Mid + Front 3D Rig */}
      <div
        style={{
          position: "relative",
          transformStyle: "preserve-3d",
          transform: `translate3d(${x}px, ${y}px, 0) scale(${scale}) rotateX(${rotateX}deg) rotateY(${rotateY}deg) rotateZ(${rotateZ}deg)`,
          filter: `drop-shadow(${shadowX}px ${shadowY}px 48px rgba(10, 14, 24, 0.28))`,
        }}
      >
        {/* Base Phone Plane (Z = 0) */}
        <div style={{ position: "relative", transform: "translateZ(0px)" }}>
          {children}

          {/* Coupled Specular Studio Glass Sheen */}
          <div
            style={{
              position: "absolute",
              inset: 0,
              borderRadius: 52,
              overflow: "hidden",
              pointerEvents: "none",
              zIndex: 40,
            }}
          >
            <div
              style={{
                position: "absolute",
                top: "-30%",
                bottom: "-30%",
                width: 260,
                left: "50%",
                transform: `translate3d(${sheenOffset}px, 0, 0) rotate(24deg)`,
                background:
                  "linear-gradient(90deg, rgba(255,255,255,0) 0%, rgba(255,255,255,0.18) 50%, rgba(255,255,255,0) 100%)",
              }}
            />
          </div>
        </div>

        {/* Front Exploded Z-Plane (+78px): Pop-Out Hero UI Cards Floating Above Glass! */}
        {explodedOverlay && (
          <div
            style={{
              position: "absolute",
              inset: 0,
              transform: "translateZ(78px)",
              transformStyle: "preserve-3d",
              pointerEvents: "none",
            }}
          >
            {explodedOverlay}
          </div>
        )}
      </div>
    </div>
  );
};
```

### Recipe 2: Baseline-Masked Kinetic Typography (`<MaskedWords />`)
Never fade/scale an entire multi-line paragraph as one rigid block. Reveal words from invisible baseline clip-boxes (`overflow: "hidden"`) with subtle rotation and harmonic stagger:

```tsx
export const MaskedKineticHeadline: React.FC<{
  words: Array<{ text: string; accent?: string; italic?: boolean }>;
  startFrame?: number;
  fontSize?: number;
  color?: string;
}> = ({ words, startFrame = 0, fontSize = 96, color = "#111318" }) => {
  const frame = useCurrentFrame();
  const { fps } = useVideoConfig();

  return (
    <div
      style={{
        display: "flex",
        flexWrap: "wrap",
        alignItems: "baseline",
        justifyContent: "center",
        columnGap: Math.round(fontSize * 0.24),
        rowGap: Math.round(fontSize * 0.08),
      }}
    >
      {words.map((w, idx) => {
        // Harmonic stagger (fast cascade, zero robotic lag)
        const delayFrames = Math.round(Math.pow(idx, 0.82) * sec(0.055, fps));
        const p = spring({
          frame: Math.max(0, frame - startFrame - delayFrames),
          fps,
          config: { damping: 24, stiffness: 210, mass: 0.78 },
        });
        const yPercent = (1 - p) * 108;
        const rot = (1 - p) * 4.5;

        return (
          <span
            key={idx}
            style={{
              display: "inline-block",
              overflow: "hidden",
              paddingBottom: "0.14em",
              marginBottom: "-0.14em",
            }}
          >
            <span
              style={{
                display: "inline-block",
                transform: `translate3d(0, ${yPercent}%, 0) rotate(${rot}deg)`,
                transformOrigin: "left bottom",
                fontFamily: w.italic ? FONT_SERIF : FONT_SANS,
                fontStyle: w.italic ? "italic" : "normal",
                fontWeight: w.italic ? 400 : 750,
                fontSize: w.italic ? Math.round(fontSize * 1.08) : fontSize,
                letterSpacing: w.italic ? "-0.015em" : "-0.045em",
                lineHeight: 1.02,
                color: w.accent ?? color,
              }}
            >
              {w.text}
            </span>
          </span>
        );
      })}
    </div>
  );
};
```

### Recipe 3: Shared-Element Container Morph (`computeContainerMorph`)
Instead of cutting from a prompt bar to a phone screen, interpolate a single container's geometry (`width`, `height`, `borderRadius`, `x`, `y`) between State A and State B:

```ts
export interface BoxGeometry {
  x: number;
  y: number;
  width: number;
  height: number;
  radius: number;
}

export const interpolateBoxGeometry = (
  a: BoxGeometry,
  b: BoxGeometry,
  progress: number
): BoxGeometry => ({
  x: a.x + (b.x - a.x) * progress,
  y: a.y + (b.y - a.y) * progress,
  width: a.width + (b.width - a.width) * progress,
  height: a.height + (b.height - a.height) * progress,
  radius: a.radius + (b.radius - a.radius) * progress,
});
```

## 6. The Ultimate Pre-Ship "Is This Alive or Is This Slop?" Self-Critique Rubric

Before accepting any generated video, look at the rendered hero and cut-boundary stills and ask these **6 ruthless questions**:

1. **The Cross-Product Clone Test:** If I hide the text and logo, does this look like a reskinned clone of `T3CodeLaunch` or a generic B2B SaaS template? *(If yes → redesign the staging, world polarity, and camera choreography to match the product's unique medium!)*
2. **The Clipart Ban Test:** Did I draw any real-world objects (coffee grinders, cars, hands, rooms) using raw inline `<svg>` geometric shapes that look like wireframe clip-art? *(If yes → replace immediately with rich layered UI cards, interactive maps/passes/schedules, topographic/data shaders, or real media assets!)*
3. **The Object Permanence Test:** Do at least 3 scene transitions physically morph, zoom through a portal, or velocity-match-cut a shared focal object—or is the video just 6 separate `<Sequence>` slides cutting on hard boundaries?
4. **The Depth & Perspective Test:** Is there true 3-layer Z-depth (background typography/environment, 2.5D tilted hero device/window with specular light response, and exploded foreground UI cards popping out toward the camera)?
5. **The Contextual Origin Test:** Does every modal, answer card, and action sheet expand from the exact coordinate of the control that triggered it?
6. **The Living Hold Test:** During readability windows, is the focal copy rock-solid while the 3D camera, glass sheen, and ambient data keep breathing?
