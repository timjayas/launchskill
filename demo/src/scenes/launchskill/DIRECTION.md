# Creative Direction Brief — LaunchSkill Official Launch Video (`DIRECTION.md`)

## 0. The 15-Second Stranger Test (3-Sentence Story)
- **Hook (`0.0–3.0s`):** Inside a sleek command prompt, you run `/launchskill` with a short prompt—and the blinking vermilion typing caret physically stretches into a cinema timeline playhead (*"Prompt to cinema."*).
- **Product Proof (`3.0–15.0s`):** That playhead scrubs a live multi-track studio deck (camera choreography, spring physics curves, and `-14 LUFS` audio waveforms), dives through the viewport to prove real 2.5D camera craft, zero-template QA contact sheets, and X-ready H.264 mastering, then wipes open to show 1-line install (`npx skills add timjayas/launchskill`) across Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, and OpenCode.
- **CTA (`15.0–18.0s`):** The terminal pill contracts around the vermilion caret into the LaunchSkill geometric playhead mark with the wordmark, *"Prompts to launch videos."*, and the 1-line install command.

1. **Surface Polarity:** `83%` Warm Archival Studio Paper (`#F7F6F2` → `#EFECE5` cyclorama, subtle camera-coupled `30px` micro-dot depth) + `17%` Earned Cinema Obsidian (`#0B0B0D`) for high-contrast proof beats. Software/terminal chrome uses sleek Dark-Mode macOS surfaces (`#0E0E11`, `1px` specular top hairline, soft multi-layer shadows) for crisp figure-ground contrast on light paper. Zero blurred radial-gradient orbs.
2. **Single Primary Accent:** **Cinema Vermilion `#E5482E`** (the color of a camera REC tally, studio playhead, and director's slate) paired with pure charcoal ink (`#141413`) and neutral stone (`#6E6C67`).
3. **Corner-Radius Geometry:** Balanced studio architectural radii (`16px` decks/cards, `12px` track clips & badges, `999px` command pills).
4. **Typography Pairing:** `Inter` (`600/700/800`, tight `-0.04em` display tracking) + italic `Instrument Serif` (`400`) on 1 focal word per editorial beat + `JetBrains Mono` (`500/600`, strict `tabular-nums`) for CLI commands, SMPTE timecodes, and `-14.0 LUFS` meters.
5. **Motion Temperament:** **`taut-mechanical`** with velocity contrast — `180–260ms` `EASE_OUT_EXPO` / `SPRING_SNAPPY` snaps alternating with continuous slow camera drift and living holds.
